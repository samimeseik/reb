Interview with the workshop master (usta), 2026-10-10. Consent: form C-2026-03, recorded with permission.

Q: How long does a typical kitchen cabinet take from order to delivery?
A: Normally about ten days. If the paint booth is busy, a bit more.

Q: What happens when Mehmet is on leave?
A: The edge-bander waits. Nobody else can set it up for the 2 mm PVC. We just stack the panels.

Q: Where do orders get stuck?
A: The paint. We have one spray booth and the lacquer needs a full day to cure.
